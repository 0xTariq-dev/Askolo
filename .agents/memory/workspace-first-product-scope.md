---
name: Space-centric product scope
description: Askolo product UI uses Spaces for family and professional contexts, with Home and explicit Agent scope.
---

Askolo is not limited to families or households. A Space can represent a family or a professional team; Spaces and invitations are cornerstones of the product. Use “Space” in product-facing language. The current technical schema and API use workspace-scoped identifiers; do not silently rename that contract.

Home is a concise, factual view of the current day only, not a plot of every item or an AI-written narrative by default. Show up to three focus actions saved per user, Space, and Plan day, followed by suggestions. In All Spaces, combine eligible actions across authorized Spaces. Eligible suggestions are unfinished items in that Space's Plan for the day, that day's habit occurrences, and any overdue open task in the Space. The Email summary counts unread messages and action-needed messages (awaiting the user's reply or flagged/starred), without listing individual emails. The Calendar summary shows the day's event count and next event/time, counting multi-day events on each day they overlap. The inline Home Agent entry opens the same full Agent, not a day-limited assistant; it may start with day context but retains the Agent's broader context within the active Space or All Spaces scope and normal authorization/privacy boundaries. Other dates are available in Agenda. All Spaces is a selection in the existing Space dropdown, not a separate view or sidebar tab; it aggregates Tasks/Plan items, Calendar events, and email summary counts, labeled by Space. Agenda is the chosen broader name and combines planning items and Calendar events in one calendar object based on ReUI's `c-event-calendar-1`, preserving existing Month/Week/Day views, date navigation, Today behavior, Google Calendar sync, and event management. Today returns to the current date in Day view; the element's view dropdown changes views. All exposed settings controls work, and the new-event button opens a modal for supported event and task types. The Plan's time zone defaults from the creator's device when created; each member can override their own display time zone in Settings, so shared-Space members see their own effective local dates and times. Home layout references are examples, not fixed templates: each user can customize their layout independently for each Space. Reuse existing design-system support for card resizing and drag-and-drop ordering; predefined widgets are future scope. At the end of a Plan day, carry unfinished items with no due date forward automatically; ask individually before carrying forward an unfinished item with a due date.
Each provider account has one owning user and one workspace association when connected to Askolo. Only a Space owner or admin may promote it for access by that Space; promotion does not transfer ownership, credentials, or the workspace association. Promoted-account selection and summaries are scoped per Space. The user chose to let active Space members read full email content from promoted accounts on demand, in addition to Home summary counts. Keep the provider as source of truth and do not persist message bodies or credentials in V2. Treat sharing as read-only until send/delete/draft actions or attachments are separately approved; whether the account owner must also consent to promotion remains open. All Spaces aggregates only currently authorized Spaces on the server and returns combined plus per-Space email totals. Unread totals cover Inbox; action-needed includes latest-message-from-someone-else threads and flagged/starred messages, which may overlap. Focus selections are per user, Space, and Plan day; All Spaces combines eligible actions across authorized Spaces, with saved choices first and suggestions filling the remaining slots up to three.

**Why:** the user confirmed the full Agent scope, current-day-only Home, provider accounts owned by one user and associated with one workspace, owner/admin promotion for Space access, and full email-content reading for active Space members of promoted accounts. They also confirmed per-Space account selection, server-side All Spaces aggregation, Agenda behavior, focus choices per user/Space/Plan day, overdue open task eligibility, action-needed criteria, and per-viewer time-zone display.

**How to apply:** use Space and Home in user-facing copy, support family and professional team contexts, represent All Spaces in the Space dropdown, label aggregated items with their Space, keep Agent actions bounded to the selected scope without limiting Agent context to the current day, keep Home on today and use Agenda for other dates, combine Plan items and events in the full Calendar object with working controls and creation flows, apply each viewer's effective time zone without changing other members' display, scope Home layout and focus selections to the user-Space/day pair, aggregate only provider accounts associated with one workspace and explicitly promoted for Space access by an owner/admin while credentials remain with their owning user, allow active Space members to read full message content only for promoted accounts using provider-backed reads, do not store message bodies or credentials, and require read-only access until additional email actions are approved. Count calendar events on each selected day they overlap, auto-carry forward undated unfinished items, and require individual approval before carrying forward items with a due date. Preserve existing technical workspace identifiers unless a separate contract change is approved.
