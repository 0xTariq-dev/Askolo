---
name: Workspace-first product scope
description: Askolo is designed for both family and professional workspaces, with invitations as a core concept.
---

Askolo is not limited to families or households. It can support both families and professional teams; workspaces and invitations are cornerstones of the product.

**Why:** the user explicitly asked to document the product scope while defining the Today/Plan experience.

**How to apply:** keep workspace terminology, navigation, and planning views usable for both families and professional teams. Do not make household-only assumptions when describing workspace membership, shared work, or invitations.
