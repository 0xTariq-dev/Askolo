# Askolo — app interface walkthrough

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** Askolo keeps the day's moving pieces together, and a voice request stays under the user's control.
- **Identity:** 16:9; Askolo dark palette (`#0C141D`, `#162332`, `#E8F2F0`, `#B2C4C1`, `#9CC4F3`) and Cairo. Original logo only.
- **Hero:** Actual, privacy-safe dashboard, planner, and actions interface captures—not simulated footage of a completed action.
- **Motion:** A full-screen dashboard pullback, restrained camera moves over interface captures, and a transcript-to-confirmation graphic. Motion highlights real UI, not fake interaction.
- **Arc:** Fast orientation → slower product tour → explicit handoff from transcription to a user-confirmed proposal → concise brand close.
- **Payoff:** End with the unchanged logo and “Plan by voice. Stay in control.” rather than another UI card.

No live product operation or completed action is shown. The workflow graphic is clearly labeled **Illustrative sequence**. The original dashboard capture has its account details masked. Narration is synthetic and descriptive; it is not presented as an Askolo assistant response.

### Shot 1 / 0.00–6.00s / 6s — overview

- **Purpose:** Immediately establish that this is a tour of the Askolo app, not an abstract feature animation.
- **Exact screen copy:** “YOUR DAY, TOGETHER” from 0.2s; “Askolo” from 1.1s; “Dashboard • Planner • Actions” from 3.5s.
- **Narration:** “Askolo brings your day into one workspace.”
- **Composition/artistic idea:** The genuine full dashboard viewport fills most of the frame. A top band with the original logo and oversized headline overlays only the blank surrounding field. Start on a close crop of the dashboard's central cards, then pull back to show the sidebar and the entire captured view. The dashboard itself is not edited to show activity.
- **Choreography:** 0–0.5s snap into the screenshot; 0.6–1.4s header resolves; 1.4–4.3s pull back to the full image while a thin accent bracket traces its perimeter; 3.5–5.5s the three section names appear in sequence; 5.5–6s camera presses toward the daily cards. No empty frame.
- **Handoff:** The outgoing card edge aligns with the top of the incoming framed dashboard.
- **Assets/technique:** Existing masked full dashboard capture and unchanged Askolo logo; CSS camera transform and SVG/shape rule.
- **Sound:** Warm calm instrumental under speech; alignment is unverified until rendered.

### Shot 2 / 6.00–14.00s / 8s — dashboard

- **Purpose:** Show the actual dashboard's daily organization without claiming populated results.
- **Exact screen copy:** “A calmer view of today” at 6.3s; “Plan” at 8.0s; “Habits · Goals · Chores” at 9.3s.
- **Narration:** “The dashboard keeps your plan, habits, goals, and chores in view without switching places.”
- **Composition/artistic idea:** Real dashboard content fills the left two thirds while editorial labels occupy a clear right column. Start close on the overview, then move focus downward to the existing plan and habit cards. No fake counters.
- **Choreography:** 6–6.8s screenshot settles sharp; 7.0–8.8s camera drifts toward the plan card; 9.0s accent underline traces the visible metrics; 10.0–12.8s counter-zoom restores the whole view while the callout changes; 13.2s rule grows into a transition bar.
- **Handoff:** Accent rule and UI frame are shared; a clipped vertical opening reveals the planner crop, with the outgoing dashboard still visible during the first 0.3s.
- **Assets/technique:** Existing sanitized dashboard capture; one framed image, text, accent rule.
- **Sound:** Narration is primary; instrumental stays ducked.

### Shot 3 / 14.00–26.00s / 12s — planner and voice

- **Purpose:** Locate voice input in the real planner and accurately explain transcription versus product decisions.
- **Exact screen copy:** “CAPTURE A THOUGHT” at 14.3s; “Type or speak” at 16.1s; “AssemblyAI transcribes” at 19.3s; “Review the transcript” at 22.2s.
- **Narration:** “In the daily planner, type a thought or speak it. AssemblyAI transcribes your words; review the transcript before continuing.”
- **Composition/artistic idea:** The actual planner interface is a large right-side image. Left-side headline is spatially tied to the visible input via one accent line. Later, a large transcript review label moves above the image; do not manufacture transcript content.
- **Choreography:** 14–15s image arrives via masked frame; 16–18s controlled zoom on the input area, then settle; 19–21s an abstract waveform resolves to a straight transcription line outside the screenshot; 22–24.5s line becomes a review bracket; 25–26s it extends toward the next panel.
- **Handoff:** The line becomes the connector of the three-step illustrative sequence; current image scales away rather than simply sliding out.
- **Assets/technique:** Existing sanitized planner capture; Cairo text and simple SVG waveform.
- **Sound:** Narration and background bed; no fabricated microphone recording.

### Shot 4 / 26.00–36.00s / 10s — proposed action

- **Purpose:** Explain the one-action boundary and explicit confirmation without portraying a completed workflow.
- **Exact screen copy:** “ONE CLEAR ACTION” at 26.2s; “Transcribe → Propose → Confirm” at 28.2s; “Nothing saved until you confirm” at 31.0s; persistent small label “Illustrative sequence · interface captures do not show a completed result”.
- **Narration:** “Askolo proposes one action. Unclear requests need clarification. Nothing is saved until you confirm.”
- **Composition/artistic idea:** A large image of the actual empty Actions interface is paired with three graphic labels outside it. Arrows and sequential emphasis explain roles, not a fictional animated save. Persistent disclosure near the lower safe area.
- **Choreography:** 26–27s action list expands from the incoming line; 28–30s stages illuminate in order; 31–33s the confirmation stage becomes the focal panel; 34–35.4s pull back to show the unchanged empty-state Actions capture; 35.4–36s accent line contracts into a small mark-shaped focal point.
- **Handoff:** The accent line becomes the underline below the closing phrase.
- **Assets/technique:** Existing sanitized Actions capture and graphic stages; no simulated click, entry, or saved state.
- **Sound:** Narration remains factual. Music subtly lifts toward the closing line.

### Shot 5 / 36.00–44.00s / 8s — lockup

- **Purpose:** Resolve the tour in a simple, memorable takeaway.
- **Exact screen copy:** “Your whole life, one place” at 36.7s; “Plan by voice. Stay in control.” at 39.0s; “Askolo” at 40.6s.
- **Narration:** “One place for the next step. Plan by voice. Stay in control.”
- **Composition/artistic idea:** The three real interface images briefly align as small panels and converge into the original logo; transition to a clean dark field with large copy. This is a graphic conclusion, not a product-result claim.
- **Choreography:** 36–37.5s three panels assemble; 37.5–39s images shrink behind the logo and disappear; 39–41s subtitle and logo resolve; 41–42.7s settled lockup; 42.7–44s the small accent pulse prepares the restart without blanking the frame.
- **Handoff:** Final accent pulse echoes the opening bracket; loop restarts with dashboard already visible in the opening scene.
- **Assets/technique:** Existing three screenshots and unchanged logo.
- **Sound:** Resolved instrumental ending under the last sentence.

## Variety review

The five shots alternate full-screen dashboard, asymmetric dashboard close-up, planner split, graphic/action explanation, and centered brand close. Dashboard captures appear in two adjacent shots only; subsequent shots use different source imagery and framing. All on-screen product states remain the original captures. Each shot has multiple timed changes and motion through its outgoing 15–20%; no blank color-only handoff is scripted.
